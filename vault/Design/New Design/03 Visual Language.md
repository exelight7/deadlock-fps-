Part of the Phase 1 design ([[01 Principles]]). **The machine-readable source is `docs/design-prototype/tokens.json`** (and `tokens.css` for the mock-up); both and the tables below are generated by `scripts/dev/design_tokens.py --write`, so they cannot drift apart. Phase 2 loads the JSON ([[06 Handoff to Build]]).

## How tokens are named
`group.role.variant`, lower case, dots: `color.bg.surface`, `space.4`, `radius.md`. In CSS a dot becomes a dash (`--color-bg-surface`); in Qt the stylesheet generator replaces `{color.bg.surface}`. Code never writes a colour, size or duration that is not a token.

**How the surfaces stack.** `color.bg.sidebar` (the sidebar and the window caption) sits beside `color.bg.app` (the page). Cards are `color.bg.surface` with a `color.border.subtle` line. Anything floating (sheet, dialog, menu, toast, the selected segment) is `color.bg.raised` with `color.border.default` and a shadow. Fields and wells are `color.bg.sunken` with `color.border.control`. Hover is `color.bg.hover`, pressed `color.bg.pressed`, a selected row or the current sidebar entry `color.bg.selected` / `color.accent.tint`.

**Rules.**
* `color.accent.fill` is used for: the one primary button of a view, the on-state of a switch or tick box, the filled part of a slider or progress bar, the underline of the current tab, a count badge. `color.accent.text` for links and the current sidebar entry. Nothing else is accent.
* Success, warning and danger each have a `text`, a `tint` (background) and a `border`. A badge or banner uses all three together and always carries an icon or a word.
* `color.danger.fill` is only for the confirming button of a destructive dialog. A destructive action outside a dialog is a neutral button with `color.danger.text`.
* Scrim and shadow are written `#RRGGBBAA`.

**Accent options.** `tokens.json` → `accents` holds two alternatives that replace eight slots each: **violet** and **orange**. Blue is the recommendation: it is the only one that cannot be confused with warning (amber) or danger (red), and it is the conventional colour of links and focus. The choice is the user's ([[Handoff - Phase 1 Design (Fable 5.2)]], decisions).

## Type
| Role | Token | Size | Weight | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | `font.size.display` | 28 | 600 | one big number (the performance level) |
| Title | `font.size.title` | 20 | 600 | the page name, a stat number |
| Subtitle | `font.size.subtitle` | 16 | 600 | card and dialog titles, the brand |
| Body | `font.size.body` | 14 | 400, 500 for labels of buttons, tabs, row titles | everything you read |
| Small | `font.size.body-sm` | 13 | 400 | descriptions under a title, table headers, monospace data |
| Caption | `font.size.caption` | 12 | 400, 500 in badges | help under a field, badges, the version |

* **Family:** `font.family.ui` = IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic and IBM Plex Sans for Latin (both SIL OFL 1.1, the same design family, so the two scripts match in weight and height). `font.family.mono` = IBM Plex Mono for data that must read exactly: paths, file names, setting names, addresses.
* **These fonts are not in the repository yet.** Adding them means downloading three font files, which needs the user's yes. Until then the stack falls back to the bundled Noto Sans Arabic / Noto Sans, then Segoe UI, and the design still holds; the mock-up shows whichever of these the viewer's PC has. Reem Kufi is no longer used.
* Line height `font.line.body` 1.5 for Latin, `font.line.arabic` 1.7 when the interface language is Arabic, `font.line.tight` 1.25 for titles.
* Numbers in tables use tabular figures and are always left-to-right. No capitals-only labels, no letter-spacing, no italics.
* **Text size setting:** 90, 100, 115, 130 % (`scale.options`). The factor multiplies every `font.size.*`, `size.control.*`, `size.icon.*` and `space.*`; borders and radii stay.

* **Spacing:** page gutter `space.6`; gap between cards `space.4`; card padding `space.5`; gap inside a card `space.3`; gap between a label and its control `space.1`.
* **Radius:** `radius.sm` small buttons, menu items, tooltips; `radius.md` buttons, fields, banners, sidebar entries, tiles; `radius.lg` cards and dialogs; `radius.pill` badges, chips, the game pill, switches. One family of round corners; no cut corners anywhere.
* **Heights:** controls 36 (`size.control.md`), compact 28 in tables and banners, large 44 for the one call to action of an empty page. Sidebar entries are 40, table rows at least 44.
* **Layout:** sidebar 216; top bar 56; pages of cards and forms cap their column at `size.content.max` 1120 and stay at the leading side; tables and the mod grid use the full width. The minimum window is 940 × 680: content is then 724 wide, which fits three mod cards, a five-column table, or two cards side by side.

## Depth
Borders and tone, not shadows. Only four things float and get a shadow: Dialog (`shadow.dialog` + `color.shadow`), Sheet (same), Menu and Toast (`shadow.popover`). In Qt this is a `QGraphicsDropShadowEffect` on those four widgets only. A dialog dims the whole window with `color.bg.scrim`; a sheet does not dim.

## Icons
One set, redrawn for this design: 20 × 20 grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fill, coloured by the text colour around them. 31 icons exist as path data in `docs/design-prototype/app.js` (`ICONS`) and are shown in `components.html`. `chev`, `back` and `ext` mirror in right-to-left; the others do not (a tick, a clock and a magnifier are not mirrored). Sizes `size.icon.sm` 16, `md` 20, `lg` 24. Flags on the Servers page stay the existing files in `assets/flags/`.

## Motion
| Token | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `motion.fast` | 120 ms | hover, pressed, switch, tick |
| `motion.base` | 180 ms | tab and page change (a fade of the content only), menu, toast in |
| `motion.slow` | 260 ms | sheet slide, dialog in, progress |
| `motion.ease` | cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) | everything |

Nothing moves while idle except a spinner, an indeterminate progress bar and a loading skeleton. No intro. With "Less motion" on (or the system setting), all three durations are 0 and the skeleton stops pulsing.
